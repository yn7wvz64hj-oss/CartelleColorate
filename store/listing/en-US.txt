Name: Nova Prism
Short description: Personal colors, images and styles for your Windows folders.

Description:
Give your folders a unique identity with Nova Prism. Pick an exact color, create your own palette or turn a PNG image into a custom folder icon.

Rotate the three-dimensional preview with your mouse and explore your favorite colors through an animated palette. Open Nova Prism from a folder's context menu or from Start.

Features:
- Custom colors, precise color picker and screen eyedropper.
- Named palettes and saved styles.
- Custom icons from PNG images and visual personalization.
- Multiple-folder editing, renaming, history and undo.
- Light and dark interface, animations and 3D preview.
- Palette import and export.
- All features free, with no Nova Prism account required.

Changes apply to folders you select. File Explorer may take a moment to refresh an icon. Protected folders and folders without write permissions cannot always be customized.
